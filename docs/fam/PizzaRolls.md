<!-- (dl (section-meta Pizza Rolls)) -->

<!-- (dl (# Ingredients)) -->

12- to 14-inch tortillas (quantity depends on pepperoni size; use enough to fill the pan)
Shredded Mozzarella Cheese
1 Jar of Spaghetti Sauce
1 Package of pepperoni
9x13 Cake Pan
Butter or cooking spray

<!-- (dl (# Instructions)) -->

1. Preheat oven to 350F degrees

<!-- (dl (## Per Tortilla)) -->

1. Spread 1 tablespoon of Spaghetti sauce to the top of tortilla.
2. Put a line of 4 or 5 pepperoni slices down the center
3. Sprinkle Mozzarella cheese over top 
4. Roll until the finished roll is about the diameter of a pepperoni slice.
5. Put in Pan.

<!-- (dl (## Once Pan is Full)) -->

1. Add a small amount of cooking spray or butter to the top of the rolls.
2. Cook for 20 minutes
