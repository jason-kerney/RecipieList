All amounts are for 25lbs turkey.

- 1 stick softened butter
- 3 tablespoons of spices total, with cumin making up half the blend. Mix the remaining spices to taste.
  - Curry
  - Ginger
  - Black or white pepper ground
  - Red pepper flakes
  - Mace
  - Sage
  - Oregano
  - Thyme
  - Lawry's seasoning salt
  - Celery seed
  - Garlic Powder
  - Onion Powder
  - Cayenne pepper powder
- 3/8ths cup clover honey
- 1 can of Cold Guinness Stout

Instructions:

1. Mix spices, honey, and butter thoroughly.
2. Gently slide your fingers between the skin and meat to loosen the skin, taking care not to tear or remove it. Leave the skin attached and covering the turkey.
3. Rub about three-quarters of the mixture onto the turkey meat under the skin. Rub the remainder into the turkey cavity (do not stuff).
4. Place turkey into oven bag, and into large pan breast down. Ensure that bag opening is easy to get to.
5. Bake turkey for half the recommended cooking time, using the temperature specified in the turkey's cooking instructions.
6. Remove turkey, and carefully open bag.
7. Pour Cold Guinness Stout over turkey
8. Reseal bag.
9. Cook for the remaining half of the recommended cooking time, plus 30 minutes.
